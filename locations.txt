Locations for Lincoln's Quest (9th Birthday, 2026)
==================================================

Hiding Spots & Audio Scripts
-----------------------------

1. Under blue pot plant at top of inside stairs
   Code: 2468

   Clue 1 (Clue1.mp3):
   "Seek the vessel of deepest blue, standing guard at the summit of the inner steps.
   Lift it, young seeker, and claim what lies beneath."


2. Inside towel cupboard at the bottom of the stairs (where Lincoln likes to hide)
   Code: 7351

   Clue 2 (Clue2.mp3):
   "You know this hiding place well, Lincoln — for you yourself have sheltered here among
   the towels. Go now to the cupboard at the foot of the stairs. Your next secret awaits within."


3. Inside a Poké Ball
   Code: 4812

   Clue 3 (Clue3.mp3):
   "The great orb of red and white — a magical sphere said to hold creatures of extraordinary
   power. Open it... and you shall find your next clue."


4. Lego Ninjago book — on the page about Jungle Kai
   Code: 6237

   Clue 4 (Clue4.mp3):
   "Seek out the great tome that names and chronicles the warriors of Ninjago. Turn its pages,
   young seeker, until you find the jungle warrior — Kai himself. There, your next code lies hidden."


5. On a skateboard
   Code: 1584

   Clue 5 (Clue5.mp3):
   "The brave and the bold glide through the streets upon four wheels. Find this trusty board
   and claim the code that rests upon it."


6. Inside Spider-Man toothbrush case in the bottom drawer of the upstairs bathroom
   Code: 5813

   Clue 6 (Clue6.mp3):
   "In the upper chamber of washing, search the lowest drawer. You will find a case bearing
   the mark of the great spider warrior. Your clue lies within."


7. In a bird box
   Code: 3175

   Clue 7 (Clue7.mp3):
   "A small wooden house, built for winged visitors of feather and song. Look inside — for a
   different kind of visitor has left something there for you."


8. Under the table on the verandah
   Code: 6284

   Clue 8 (Clue8.mp3):
   "Step beyond the walls to the covered realm outside. Beneath the table upon the verandah,
   your secret rests in the shadows."


9. Under Socrates (made of socks)
   Code: 7182

   Clue 9 (Clue9.mp3):
   "Seek the curious creature born not of flesh and bone, but of the humble sock. He bears the
   name of a great thinker from the ancient world. Look beneath him, and you shall find your prize."


10. On a trumpet
    Code: 4861

    Clue 10 (Clue10.mp3):
    "The brass voice of music calls out across the realm! Find the mighty instrument that rings
    with a single breath — and upon it, your final clue awaits."

     quest-seeker. But before I reveal your prize... I must ask. Are you... TRULY... ready?"
    [Yes / No buttons appear on screen — record silence or a dramatic pause here]

    "...Of course you are! I was going to tell you either way. Now listen carefully, young

     rightfully yours. Go now, Lincoln. Your quest is complete. Happy Birthday."
